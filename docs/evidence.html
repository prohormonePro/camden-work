Evidence, with its limits

Reviewed September 24, 2026. This page separates release proof, named tests, maintainer runs and research specifications. Machine-readable claims and schema retain exact source/test bindings and falsifiers. A passing reference validator is not a certification of semantic truth.

Released baseline

Camden Work v0.1.0: commit f8ffad19a198915564b9816e156b22b3fd4dddee. Documentation baseline: b48a377b9675dc64a03b0957fe5e56d4c8fac1e8. Public release CI and the September 23 addendum retain their original scope. The 84 release-suite tests, 18 overlapping installed checks and seven challenge predicates have different denominators.

The seven challenge predicates are crash_after_target_commit, one_original_effect, unknown_observed, retry_fenced, independent_task_verified, registered_wait_serviced, and unsafe_baseline_duplicates. The two child exits are 72. One original effect is not the total target row count. The naive baseline uses new identities and is not an industry benchmark.

Source and negative tests

tests/test_finalization.py rejects export without adoption, adoption without delivery, altered inbox bytes, enlarged work scope and lost current visibility. tests/test_continuity.py exercises conditional compensation and fresh verification. These narrow mappings are PUBLIC_SOURCE_AND_TEST_MAPPED, not evidence that every larger gauntlet row ran together.

New observed local run

The separately identified source-tree benchmark has four started Windows episodes: two cold-controller and two recovery-of-recovery. All four reached BUSINESS_COMPLETE within their declared local boundary. Exact exits were [72,0] and [72,73,0]. Both predeclared obligations had one effect; original budgets/deadlines and attempts survived; old owner use was rejected; local adoption, delivery bytes and finalization passed.

This is MAINTAINER_REPORTED with raw manifest and every episode. The plan binds actual source SHA-256, runtime, storage, repetitions and fault schedule. No unsuccessful started episode was omitted. The runner is reproducible from source; the immutable released wheel is unchanged.

B_e and D_e were zero within those synthetic runs. A_e was two or three harness launches per episode. Setup human time was NOT_MEASURED. Automatic restart, background wake, remote effects and genuinely new business decisions were not exercised. No autonomous-supervisor claim follows from these observations.

What could disprove the claims

Find a duplicate original effect, a stale owner accepted at the effect boundary, lost or reset attempt/deadline state, independent eligible work blocked, or a local READY with missing adoption/delivery/current evidence under the stated trust model. A correctly configured alternative meeting the same contract defeats an exclusivity claim. Related work has source review only; no comparative run exists.

The catalog has 283 research mechanisms. The versioned differentiation overlay maps six narrow relationships; all other mappings remain UNKNOWN. A mapping is neither a whole-mechanism certification nor a count of prevented failures.
